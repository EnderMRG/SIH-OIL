## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an editorial industrial aesthetic, fusing the disciplined precision of geological and architectural engineering with the stark authority of high-end Swiss and brutalist print publication. Tailored for enterprise energy intelligence, subsurface geotechnical analysis, and specialized technical operations, it communicates absolute certainty, uncompromised precision, and intellectual rigor.

The visual dialect pairs an architectural warm off-white canvas with deep carbon black typography, punctuated by deliberate moments of warm mustard graphic geometry. The interplay between massive high-contrast serif display typography and utilitarian grotesque sans-serif micro-labels bridges archival fieldwork and cutting-edge digital telemetry. It rejects glossy consumer tropes in favor of tactile paper-like backgrounds, refined hairline dividers, and high-density information architecture.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, archival tone grounded in warm neutrals and stark contrast. 

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces**: The base environment relies on warm, architectural paper hues: `#EDECE8` (primary canvas), `#F4F3EF` (elevated white/panel surface), and `#E5E4DE` (secondary sub-surface containers).
- **Ink & Typography**: Deep carbon black (`#0D0D0D`) serves as the primary ink for all authoritative headlines, primary interactive states, and solid action pills. Secondary copy and technical metadata use muted slate umber (`#6E6B65`).
- **Graphic Accent**: Warm mustard yellow (`#E5B83B` / `#DFAC24`) is reserved strictly for graphic balance marks, focal point indicators, and deliberate editorial punctuation.
- **Hairlines & Outlines**: Subtle, precise dividers utilize `#DCDAD4` to demarcate sections without introducing heavy visual weight.
- **Alert States**: Muted industrial warning states balance high readability with low-saturation amber-tinted backings and primary black typography.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is defined by high tension between classical editorial grandeur and technical grotesque precision.

1. **Editorial Display Layer (EB Garamond)**: Large titles are set in tightly tracked, all-caps or title-case serif with bold weights. This establishes immediate editorial authority reminiscent of architectural treatises and technical monographs.
2. **Operational Sans Layer (Space Grotesk)**: Body copy, interactive navigation, metadata, and data tables use a crisp, geometric grotesque. Navigation items and system architecture tags are strictly capitalized with generous letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to guarantee legibility across technical telemetry dashboards.
3. **Data & Indicators**: Dates, coordinates, and version badges utilize monospace or fixed-width grotesque attributes to emphasize instrumentation and data fidelity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model divides the viewport into deliberate asymmetric structural panels, contrasting an open, generous editorial hero stage with a structured, modular operational sidebar.

- **Grid Architecture**: A dual-zone desktop composition featuring a flexible primary stage (spanning approximately 65-72% of the viewport) coupled with an anchored utility rail (28-35% width). 
- **Rhythm & Padding**: Content follows a strict 4px baseline grid. Section gaps use wide intervals (`space-xl` / 40px) to give expansive breathing room to monumental serif typography, while component internals maintain dense, functional grouping (`space-xs` to `space-md`).
- **Breakpoints & Reflow**:
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: Two-column asymmetrical view with fixed right utility panel and unified vertical scroll.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: Side-by-side collapses into a stacked hierarchy; utility widgets reflow into a 2-column sub-grid below primary telemetry.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: Canvas margins step down to `1.25rem` (`20px`). Large serif headers step down via `display-hero-mobile`, and tags flow into horizontally scrollable or wrapping pill trays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through tonal layering and razor-sharp hairlines rather than diffused drop shadows:

- **Flat Architectural Tiers**: Surfaces rely on color steps to communicate depth. The baseline canvas sits at `#EDECE8`, primary cards lift forward via solid `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F3EF`, and interactive slots rest within `#E5E4DE`.
- **Zero-Shadow Philosophy**: Diffuse box shadows are completely excluded. Depth is mechanical, tactile, and planar.
- **Hairline Framing**: Structural separation is reinforced with precise 1px borders (`#DCDAD4` or `#0D0D0D` for high-priority targets). This creates an unyielding blueprint/drafting table structure.
- **Color Overlay**: Highlighting relies on solid color blocking—pure `#0D0D0D` fills for active or focal pills and muted tints for alerts.

## Shapes

The design system employs a calculated combination of generous radii and pure capsule geometries:

- **Container Radii**: Cards and primary display containers use `rounded-xl` (20px to 24px) providing an architectural, molded-slab quality that softens technical density.
- **Pill Shapes (Full Capsule)**: All interactive buttons, status badges, dropdown triggers, and system navigation links are fully rounded capsules (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Geometric Accents**: Floating non-standard oval and circular geometries (e.g., the signature mustard oval mark and circular thumbnail framing) act as organic counterweights to the orthogonal structure.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Pills
- **Primary Pill**: Carbon black background (`#0D0D0D`), stark white text (`#FFFFFF`), fully pill-shaped (`radius: 9999px`), padded with `0.875rem 1.5rem`. Accompanied by integrated trailing icons (e.g., chevron or arrow) aligned to the right edge.
- **Secondary / Architecture Nav Pills**: Pill-shaped capsules with an off-white background (`#FFFFFF` or `#F4F3EF`), subtle border (`#DCDAD4`), and uppercase grotesque labels (`11px`, tracking `0.08em`). Active state inverts to `#0D0D0D` fill with white typography.

### Cards & Content Modules
- **Modular Info Cards**: Radii of 20px–24px with solid paper-white backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`), wrapped in a 1px border (`#DCDAD4`). Internal margins use `1.5rem`.
- **Media Previews**: Enclosed imagery features soft internal rounding (`16px`), paired directly with micro-headline titles and clean sans-serif editorial copy.

### Lists & Row Items
- **Subsurface Data Rows**: Minimal horizontal list items separated by a subtle 1px divider (`#DCDAD4`). Each row includes:
  - Circular avatar/thumbnail slot (`40px x 40px`).
  - Title and metadata vertically stacked (`Space Grotesk`, bold title with muted secondary status).
  - Right-aligned tabular timestamp (`12 MAY, 2024`).
  - Action trigger (triple-dot overflow or direct arrow).

### Hazard & System Alerts
- **Asterisk Alert Pill**: Full-width or inline banner with a light warm neutral backdrop, framed with hairline boundaries and an industrial asterisk icon (`*`) indicating system alerts. Uses bold uppercase label text to communicate immediate operational hazards.

### Badges & Year Anchors
- **Archival Box Stamp**: Small square or rectangular micro-containers (`rounded: 4px`) carrying structural framing lines, housing stacked metadata such as the catalog year (`20 / 24`).
